## The person page's first card, redesigned (#3131)

The owner, 2026-11-19: the profile card "looks ugly" and "messy". What made it so, measured on his notebook:
a citation number after every value, each fact a label stacked over its value, a seven-row column of aliases next
to a four-row column, three button styles in the header, and a translation strip floating outside the card.

- **Header:** three lines. The name; role · organisation · units; place · last contact · mail · "N in Today".
  A 64 px face with its initials centred.
- **One action column, top right:** 调查 with its ⋯, then the page's translation state as one quiet line. Every
  person page puts its controls there.
- **Facts:** one line each, label column then value, flowing into two balanced columns. Aliases and languages sit on
  one line; a list of more than two values shows two and "+N more". Addresses and handles stay on one line and copy
  on a click. Facts the header already says are not repeated.
- **Quieter:** sources are one hover (or keyboard focus) away instead of a number after every value; there are no
  group headings or label icons; what is unknown is in the card's tooltip, not a "N not found" line; internal tags
  are not shown.
