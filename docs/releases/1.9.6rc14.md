# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc14 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**. Python **3.11+**.

## REM reader: organisations are a record to browse

- **The Organisations list is a table, like People.** Columns: organisation (logo or monogram),
  kind, what they do or mean to you, website (one click; when unknown, a contact's business email
  domain, marked *Derived*), location, people known there, activity (messages in 90 days) and last
  contact, newest first. Every column sorts, kind chips filter, and **Columns** adds first contact,
  legal entity and parent. On a phone each organisation is a labelled card. A university's units
  sit under it behind a disclosure (#3037).
- **An organisation page reads like a page of a book.** One centred column: an opening paragraph
  that says what the organisation is, what it is to you, how many people you know there and since
  when; the people and structure as a tree (organisation → units, courses or teams → people with
  their roles, #2992); the relationship's dated milestones; connections; labelled facts with a
  clickable website; full memory and sources at the foot (#3036).
- **No to-dos on organisations.** The list and the page carry no Today counts, waiting groups,
  overdue chips, reply-speed lines, update badges or contact charts. To-dos live in Today (#3036).

## REM reader: Knowledge and headings

- **One Knowledge place.** The rail keeps **Knowledge** (topic entries and the graph) and drops
  **All records**; old All records links land on Knowledge, and anything only it reached is
  reachable from Knowledge or search. A notebook with one or two topics links to its graph instead
  of looking empty (#2996).
- **Pages lose the sentences that explained their order** (Sessions' subtitle, Projects' sort
  sentence and board preface); the layout carries it (#2994).

## REM reader: Projects

- **Projects open on a board by state**: Active, Waiting, Quiet, Done; an empty column is a narrow
  header. A project page leads with its state, up to three next steps and recent activity; purpose,
  findings, people, history, related records and the graph fold under **Full memory** (#2993).
  Related records are now one click further away than before.

## Known limits

- Logos show as monograms until the night stores them (#3038, not in this release).
- Project next steps are shown as the page records them, often a quoted request rather than a short
  action.

## Upgrade

```bash
uv tool install --force 'connectonion==1.9.6rc14' --with onionwright --prerelease allow
```
