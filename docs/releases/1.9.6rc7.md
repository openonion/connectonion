# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc7 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**. Python **3.11+**. Dependency floors are unchanged.

## REM reader

- **Three ways to close a row, in words.** Every Today row ends with **Mark done** (标为完成),
  **Not now** (暂不处理) and **Ignore** (忽略), on screen without a hover. Mark done covers what was
  settled offline; Not now leaves Today until new mail arrives on the thread; Ignore is for an ad or a
  notification and never comes back. Each notice says what happened and keeps **Undo** (#2977).
- **Stop an advertiser for good.** When everything a row cites came from one machine or bulk sender,
  Ignore's notice offers **Stop showing mail from** *address*. One tap keeps that sender out of Today
  and out of the night's reply drafts. The list is `.state/ignored-senders.txt` in your notebook,
  one line per sender, and **Settings → Ignored senders** lets any of them back. REM never stops a
  sender by itself and never offers a person's address (#2977).
- **Old promises stop shouting.** A promise more than 60 days past its date, with a thread quiet for
  as long, is no longer counted among the people waiting and is no longer red; Today asks once
  whether it is over. The night counts on Today agree with each other, the phone composer no longer
  covers the card, and a quiet night chart gives its row back (#2977).
- **⌘K and ? open again right after Esc.** Closing the search or the shortcuts sheet and opening it
  straight away used to do nothing (#2977).
- **Every record has a real name.** Topics no longer appear under a generic heading such as "The
  draft"; a record with nothing better shows its file's readable name, and organisations take the
  name their own page gives instead of a bare domain (#2970).
- **Record pages show the record.** An organisation page is no longer hidden whole because one line
  is private: only labelled passages are, each with its own reveal. A page no longer waits forever on
  "翻译中" (the original is marked 原文), the map sits below its tabs, and project summaries leave out
  local paths (#2973).
- **The Skills page fits on a screen.** The skills you use most come first, with how often you used
  them in the last 180 days; the rest fill the width as an index with one plain line each, and one
  filter replaces the two search boxes (#2975).

There is nothing to do: install this release and run `co rem open`.
