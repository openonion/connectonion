# 1.9.0a8 — what 1.9.0a7's acceptance run found

An opt-in preview of 1.9.0. Stable is 1.8.9. Everything here was found by
running 1.9.0a7 on a copy of the owner's notebook.

- **No more paying for the same refusal.** 1.9.0a7 stopped the loop for project
  pages; people and organisation pages near the 20k limit still went round it,
  refused and retried on the same mail (643k and 933k tokens). Now every
  one-page turn is told the page's size and the limit before it starts, and a
  refused investigation waits until new mail arrives. A timeout or crash is
  still retried (#2041).
- **The budget meter moves.** "0 of 10 points" stayed at 0 after millions of
  tokens, because Codex's weekly meter reports whole percents and each run used
  less than one. A run now counts the meter's move when there is one, and
  otherwise its fresh tokens: a point is a million (input minus cached, plus
  output). Investigations you start by hand count too (#1990).
- **Investigations read what init already saved.** An unfinished mail archive
  was ignored until it completed; now its saved bodies are read, coverage says
  how many of how many are saved, and resuming the archive prints its progress
  (#2042).
- **`co rem sync` ends on a summary**: one line per page as it finishes, then
  what was new, refused and left, the tokens, and where the full record is,
  instead of a field dump of about 200 lines (#2044).
- Every run's tokens land in a usage stage, so the stages add up to the total
  (#2043); the queue counts a page written by sync as read, and an organisation
  counts only people worth investigating (#2046); the status line keeps short
  source labels (#2045, in part).
