# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc15 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**. Python **3.11+**.

## REM reader: organisations

- **The Organisations table shows the tree, expanded.** A university's programmes, courses and teams
  sit under it with guide lines, and its name cell says how many units and people are under it.
  **By structure / Flat** switches views; sorting by another column or filtering by kind flattens the
  table and shows each unit as «Parent › Unit». Collapses are remembered (#3037).
- **On real notebooks:** a long summary is clamped to two lines in the table; an unknown is never
  printed as a fact (no «Unknown, not investigated yet» as a page's opening or a connection's
  description); no activity shows a dash; kind chips offer **Unclassified**; an organisation's
  opening states the first and latest contact dates its people and mail record (#3037, #3036).

## REM reader: Today and Projects

- **«Talk it over» shows its conversation.** The agent answered, but the page reloaded and forgot
  which conversation was open, so nothing appeared. The reader now reopens the same conversation
  after the reload, with the context, the reply and the composer.
- A card under «waiting on you» leads with what you owe and counts only that (#2947).
- A project's open items share one frame with aligned actions; the board switch stays on one row on
  a phone; a step's owner is labelled as an agent request, an unconfirmed owner or an available
  answer instead of «Agent» for all three (#3042).
- A private day you revealed on Sessions stays revealed when the page redraws.

## REM: facts

- Last contact counts only messages from the person or from you, not a third party's group message
  (#2951). A project's Last activity is the newest dated source on its page (#2952). A shared
  notification sender that carries many people is never offered to stop (#2978).

## Core

- `lark-oapi` ships with connectonion, so `co lark` and `co feishu` work on a fresh install (#2985).

## Tests

- The reader's Chrome suite is green (758 tests); seven were red on main (#2868). The host-leak
  guard reads `ps` output cut mid-character (#3041). The upgrade test stops the Host it started
  (#2980).
