# 1.9.6rc36

Release candidate for 1.9.6. Stable remains 1.9.5.

REM: the rc35 bug hunt's fixes (#3165).

## Web sources (#3200)
- A web source row names the page and its site instead of a URL-encoded query; a search reads as the search words.
- Its card is never empty: the page title, the site, the day REM read it, what it said, and "Open the web page ↗". With
  nothing saved, one honest line plus the link.
- Investigations now keep each web page's title.

## Today and the calendar (#3198)
- Today's side column stays inside its width: long event titles end in an ellipsis and the page never scrolls sideways.
- Today's calendar speaks Chinese in the Chinese reader (接下来, 完整日程).
- On a phone, a day's deadlines fold into one "N 个期限" line under its events, opened in place.
- The translation count has words ("373 页待翻译", "373 untranslated"), never a bare number.
- A mailbox described as one in its Role line is not listed as a person; a person who manages a mailbox still is.

## Privacy (#3199, #2840)
- A curtain inside a sentence is one inline bar on the sentence's line, as long as the passage (capped), and wraps like
  a word; whole-block curtains are unchanged.
- `co rem status` hides sensitive passages as `[hidden]` and shows links by host; `--show-private` prints them in full.

## Dates and ids, the rest (#3201)
- A prose date right before a citation chip with the same day prints once.
- One date form on a page: "8 月 10 日" and "9/3" read 8月10日 and 9月3日; links, buttons and headers go through the same pass.
- Short commit hashes become a "提交" chip and longer hex ids a "记录" chip, full id on hover; a source titled by a hex id
  reads by its kind and day.

## Phone
- An open source sheet is not covered by the tab bar.
