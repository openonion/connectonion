# ConnectOnion 1.9.7rc11 (release candidate)

Stable is **1.9.6**. Python **3.11+**.

- **The Today reply card, rebuilt to one spec** (#3324, closes #3303 and #3302, spec #3312): 发送 is the only filled
  button; the row's other actions sit under ⋯. The letter reads in full, without an inner scroll window, and folds
  with 展开全文 past about 14 lines (8 on a phone). A line under it names what REM read ("REM 读了 <date> 的〈<subject>〉，
  起草了这封回复"). ‹ i / n › is always visible; J/K and a swipe on a phone step through the cards.
- **Honest channels** (#3302): a reply keeps to the channel the message came in on. Another channel is offered only
  when the person's page cites a chat on it or names a handle there. A draft already stored on a channel the person
  is not reachable on (one Outlook thread had been drafted as Lark) is refused at send and offered 改写成邮件. The
  badge, footer and full screen all take their channel from the draft.
- **Send says what happened** (#3303): Send never looks disabled. With an open question it names it and scrolls to
  it; otherwise 发送中… appears at once, then 已发送 with undo, or the Host's reason with 重试.
- **Full screen** (#3303): a title bar "回复 <name> · <channel>" with × at the top right; Esc closes, and a click outside
  asks once if the letter changed. Open questions are clickable chips in the letter and are answered at the top of
  the right column. On a phone the header is one bar: 取消 · 回复 <name> · <channel> · 发送.

Known: the provider's own colours (Outlook blue, Gmail red) are not in the palette yet (#3328). The typed choices for
times and yes/no answers land separately (#3301).
