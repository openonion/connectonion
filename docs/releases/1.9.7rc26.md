## Agent email attachments

Send a file with `co email send alice@example.com "Report" "See attached." --attach report.pdf`.
Repeat `--attach` for up to 20 files and 10 MiB total. `co email read 123` lists
received attachment IDs. `co email download 123 1 --out report.pdf` saves one
attachment to a new path.

Python callers can pass `attachments` to `send_email()` and use
`get_email()` and `download_email_attachment()` for received mail. Attachment
delivery requires the corresponding hosted mail backend.
