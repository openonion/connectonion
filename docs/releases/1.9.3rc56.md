# ConnectOnion 1.9.3rc56 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.2**; Python **3.11+** and the existing dependency floors are retained.

1.9.3rc55 was tagged but never published: its release workflow failed because a fresh
`co init --yes` opened `codex login` in a browser and waited. This candidate carries everything
rc55 described (Today as one queue, replies shown as the mail they become, a fixed drafting skill
in your own voice, the full-screen writer, the Mail/Lark/WhatsApp switch; see
[1.9.3rc55](1.9.3rc55.md)) plus the fixes below.

## Decisions stick, and the page opens at once (#2865, #2861)

- **Mark done** and **Skip** are remembered by the notebook, not by one browser: the next night's
  round and every other device see them, and an item you skipped returns only when new mail
  arrives on that thread, dated as such. Undo works for five seconds inline and permanently from
  the item.
- The reader shows its shell and the first cards before the whole notebook is parsed; a 900-page
  notebook paints in about a second instead of after a seven-second scan.
- A page without a Chinese version keeps its words in the Chinese interface; opening it in the
  live reader queues a translation once and says so under the title. (An earlier build of this
  candidate replaced such pages with "Translating" everywhere; that never reached a release.)

## Plain words, one date grammar (#2864)

- Dates read the same everywhere: today, yesterday, N days ago, then the day, with the year only
  when it differs. Debt ages and due dates sit in one place on the card, not repeated in the prose.
- Headlines are the sentence that matters, citations are superscripts, labels say what a thing is
  (Outlook reply, Gmail reply, Lark, session) before you click, and session previews name the work
  without local paths. Copy-a-command controls left the primary surfaces; they live under reading
  settings.

## The schedule holds steady (#2870)

- A turn that overruns is ended with its whole process tree, so an orphaned `co rem next` no longer
  holds the maintenance lock for hours; a busy notebook has a ceiling and the next fire continues.
- Failed or blocked turns are visible on Today and in `co rem status` with their receipt, not only in
  the log.
- `co init` and `co rem start` in a terminal print the consent summary, the undo block and the
  estimate, then proceed; they no longer pause. Neither opens a browser: when Codex is not signed
  in, init prints the exact login command and `co rem start` as the next step. A clean init takes
  about four seconds.

## Drafting

- The `rem-reply-draft` skill reads raw samples of your own mail beside the two identity pages and
  may decide that **no reply is needed**, which the card shows as the reason instead of a mail
  composer; such an item cannot be sent or opened in a provider draft, and Done/Undo keeps that
  verdict.

## Known limits

- The opt-in Chrome suite (`CO_REM_BROWSER_TEST=1`) is green on this candidate but is not a CI gate
  (#2868).
- Sending and rewriting work in the live reader on this computer only; a static snapshot says so on
  the card.

## Install after publication

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.3rc56'
```
