# ConnectOnion 1.9.7rc6 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.6**. Python **3.11+**.

- **WeChat seeds people into co rem (#3281, #3279).** Building the map now creates a people page for each
  one-to-one WeChat contact (the WeChat id is the handle, so investigation reads the chat in); a contact who
  also emails merges by name; groups and official accounts are left out. The night seeds new WeChat contacts
  too, not only `co rem map`. The wechat source is on by default once `co wechat key capture` has saved keys
  (reading your own WeChat locally is reversible), and `co rem sources remove wechat` undoes it.
