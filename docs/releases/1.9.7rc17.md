Release candidate for 1.9.7. Stable remains 1.9.6.

## WeChat on an upgraded notebook (#3369, #3368)

- The first page build after an upgrade backfills each local source's chat activity and onboarding record (local,
  read-only, no model). WeChat people show their last contact, 90-day count and who wrote last without waiting for
  the night run.
- `co rem map … --source NAME` records activity and the onboarding record the way the night does, and queues the
  pages it made.
- `co rem status` / `co rem sources` count the pages a source already holds instead of saying "never mapped".
- A WeChat nickname with no letter, digit or CJK character is never a page title: the remark is used, or
  `微信好友（无名字 · …1234）`; the next map retitles existing pages.
- The People row marks only the minority source, so the few mail contacts stand out among WeChat rows.
