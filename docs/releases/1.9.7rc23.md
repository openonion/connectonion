# 1.9.7rc23

Release candidate for 1.9.7. Stable remains 1.9.6.

## Privacy: a line-leading mark is curtained at 遮敏感 (#3416, #3410)

- A line that opens with a privacy mark is shown as the standard 敏感 curtain at 遮敏感, in the header, a paragraph and a
  list item alike, and never with its raw mark. On rc22 such a line showed its words in full at the default level.
- A Chinese reader says its own labels in Chinese: a leaked section name is translated, and a page's English shows as
  its original only while the line is still English.

## Status after a fix, in local time (#3414, #3413)

- Once the fix for a page that kept stopping the night is installed, Today and `co rem status` say the next round skips
  that page, in local time, counting one night window.

## The owner's side on client organisations (#3415, #3397)

- 你这边对接的人 is found from what real pages say, so it appears on real client organisations.
