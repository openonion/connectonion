# 1.9.7rc16

Release candidate for 1.9.7. Stable remains 1.9.6.

## REM is faster on a big notebook (#3365)

- The same-person check compares only pairs that can match (same results as the full sweep, pinned by a test).
- The whole-notebook tag and census scans run once per page build and once per `co rem status`, never stale.
- On a 1,352-person notebook `co rem status` went from about 20 s to about 11 s.

## Every WeChat friend, and WeChat in the People table (#3357, #3346)

- Every address-book friend becomes a candidate person; chats are read from the keyed message databases and from
  WeChat's full-text index. The source reports `微信好友 N · 找到聊天 M · 还缺 K`.
- People columns (last contact, 90-day count, who wrote last) merge WeChat with mail; rows carry a 微信/邮件 mark and a
  来源 filter; friends without chats or quiet for 90 days fold away. The person page labels the id 微信.

## Replies on the right channel (#3358)

- A draft written for a chat the person is not on is rewritten on the thread's own channel by the next round, with a
  note on the card. Until then the card and full screen lead with 改写成邮件 and hold 发送.

## Coverage (#3354, #3356)

- `co wechat`, `co rem sources` and `co rem status` show what each source read and what it skipped, with the fix.
  Re-running `co wechat key capture` adds keys to the saved ones.
- Today shows one plain line when a source has unread data, with a button for the fix.

## Also

- Opening the reader no longer asks for translation status (#3352).
- One token sheet for states and motion; the full-screen writer dims the page in dark mode too (#3362).
- 3D graph: 95th-percentile fit inside the legend's inset, search frames the hit and its neighbours (#3342).
