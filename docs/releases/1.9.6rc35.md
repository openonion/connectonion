# 1.9.6rc35

Release candidate for 1.9.6. Stable remains 1.9.5.

REM: the rc34 bug hunt's fixes (#3165).

## Staying signed in, and landing on the new build (#3190)
- A capped night turn that accepted work is no longer counted as a failure; one that accepted nothing still is.
- Another app's cookie on 127.0.0.1 no longer signs the open reader out; the cookie survives a browser restart.
- A lost session is a styled page in your language with one button, not a bare line.
- After an upgrade, Reconnect (and an idle page by itself) waits under a quiet "switching to the new version" bar until
  the new build's page is ready, then reloads onto it. A page you are typing in is offered the reload instead.

## Today's calendar (#3188)
- An event's detail opens beside the event, never over it or the rail.
- The all-day lane holds calendar events only; notebook deadlines show as one "{n} due" count per day.
- The day timeline opens an hour above the first event or now, whichever is earlier.

## Person pages (#3131)
- Facts are said once: Full memory no longer repeats the profile card, and a fact's doubled date prints once.
- The header's place line keeps its privacy mark, so Public only hides a personal location.
- Agents, mailboxes and companies are no longer listed as people (#3157).

## Dates and ids, everywhere
- One pass after every render shows ISO dates and ranges in the reader's form, says a date once, and turns raw source
  ids into named chips that open the source (or say the original isn't in this snapshot).

## Top bar and hand-offs
- The top bar is compact: a small translation count beside 中/EN and one short privacy word on desktop; one 显示 menu on
  a phone. Nothing is truncated.
- A hand-off row shows its summary only when it is in the reader's language (#3143).

## Today's first screen
- On a desk, Today's side column opens with Next: the next three events (with a Join link for a meeting) and a 7-day
  strip. On a phone, one line above the cards leads to the calendar. The cards stay first in the work column.

## Sources
- A source note never prints a raw `[personal]` / `[sensitive]` label, at any privacy level.
- A mail citation REM indexed but never archived opens with its sender, subject and time, and says the body was not kept.
- A quote from a profile-card row reads as its values.
- A page is never listed in its own tag row.

## Night stages that drain
- A break takes a quarter of the waiting tag batches (at least 3), so a large tag backlog clears in one night.
- Statement translations that kept an English title are queued again and remade; the old translation stays readable
  until then (#3142).
