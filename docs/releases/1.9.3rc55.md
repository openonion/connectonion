# ConnectOnion 1.9.3rc55 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.2**; Python **3.11+** and the existing dependency floors are retained.

## Today is one queue, and a reply looks like the mail it becomes (#2861, #2862)

- The reader's front page is one queue: everything that needs you, newest and due first, one
  card each, one primary action per card. The copy-prompt buttons are gone; the only quiet
  secondary is **Mark done**, which this browser alone remembers.
- The schedule drafts one reply per mail thread waiting on you (up to `limits.drafts_per_night`,
  default 10) on the `rem-reply-draft` skill: it reads who you both are (your page, their page,
  the relationship, how they write), decides on each ask in the latest message, and writes in
  your register for that relationship, using your own `email-writing` skill when one is installed
  under `~/.claude`, `~/.codex`, `~/.cursor` or `~/.co`.
- A prepared reply is shown as the mail it becomes: recipient, subject, the body in the thread's
  language, a gist in your language, and **Why this reply** (who they are, what was agreed, what
  you owe and since when). **Send** replies in the thread from your mailbox; **Write full screen**
  opens the letter to edit by hand, with that background and the thread beside it, and sends your
  text; **Edit in Gmail/Outlook drafts** creates an unsent provider draft; **Skip** parks it until
  new mail arrives. A channel switch (Mail / Lark / WhatsApp) rewrites the reply for that channel
  and sends it as you through `lark-cli` or `co whatsapp` when the notebook knows how to reach the
  person there. Sending and rewriting work in the live reader on this computer only; the click is
  the approval.
- The sidebar names the notebook after you ("<first name>'s memory") instead of its folder.

## Sources and rounds

- Attachments are read metadata-first and billing mail is routed to the right organisation
  (#2832); browser history joins the sources (#2831); relationship statistics come from the
  notebook's own index (#2843); the native round keeps its run memory (#2828); tidy moves service
  pages and prunes stale preparation (#2833).
- The sessions page frames each coding-agent session like the agent that ran it, with a composer
  to continue it.
- `co init` installs the Codex CLI when none is found, and shares the desktop app's login.

## Known limits

- The opt-in Chrome suite (`CO_REM_BROWSER_TEST=1`) is now green on this candidate; it is not yet a
  CI gate (#2868).
- Drafts for items whose original mail is not archived are not written; the card says so.

## Install after publication

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.3rc55'
```
