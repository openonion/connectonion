# ConnectOnion 1.9.3rc59 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.2**; Python **3.11+** and the existing dependency floors are retained.

## Hotfix: the claim audit completes when a cited original is not in the archive (#2879)

- After 1.9.3rc58 let the stuck page task through the retention check, the same task stalled in
  the claim audit: the candidate cited twelve sources, the audit packet could supply eleven
  originals (one mail is not in the archive), and completion demanded a checkpoint for all twelve.
  The writer rightly refused to invent one; every 15-minute follow-up retried the same step.
- The packet now names such citations as `unavailable_originals`, the writer is told they need no
  checkpoint, completion counts only originals the packet supplied, and the audit report, the
  review and the run log record them as `sources_unavailable` — a skipped check leaves evidence it
  was skipped; the claim and its citation stay on the page. When any original was unavailable the
  audit verdict is `insufficient`, never `pass`.
- `co rem next` shows the skipped citations in its progress line.
- Everything in [1.9.3rc58](1.9.3rc58.md), [1.9.3rc57](1.9.3rc57.md) and [1.9.3rc56](1.9.3rc56.md)
  ships here.
