# ConnectOnion 1.9.3rc64 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.2**; Python **3.11+** and the existing dependency floors are retained. This is
the candidate for stable 1.9.3.

## REM does the work

- **Owed tasks run.** An approved task runs now, with full access, through ConnectOnion's own
  integrations: `co claude` (Claude Code, with your own Claude setup) or Codex. Every pending item in
  Today and Waiting has **Hand to Codex** and **Hand to Claude**. Stop is confirmed; Undo still works.
- **Replies to people you trust can go without you.** REM sets a person's direct-send tier from the
  relationship (partner, close) and never touches a tier you set. Sends go through the 10-minute hold.
- **No count caps.** The night is bounded by the weekly Codex budget, not by numbers of drafts or
  turns. A task refused twice for the same reason is parked and retried another day.
- **Translation into the languages you write.** With no languages set, REM translates into the
  languages detected from your own writing, with a share of every night.
- **Four model levels.** Each stage runs on the model its difficulty needs: summaries on level 1,
  translation and extraction on level 2, investigation and drafting on level 3, deepening, audits
  and owed tasks on level 4. `models.level1`–`level4` and `route.<stage>` override.
- **A designed morning email** to your own mailbox (`notify.channel outlook`), in your language: what
  REM prepared and sent overnight, and at most three decisions, one per person.
- **Honest status.** A mailbox not read for a day shows when it was last read; nights are counted.
- **The night schedule** replaces an old pinned default once; `schedule.profile dense` is available.

## The reader

- **Opens at once.** `co rem open` serves the last rendered page from disk and rebuilds behind it; one
  stable link per notebook, so a reload shows the newest render.
- **Fuse design.** One ink, one paper, four sizes. People and organisations are records: the name at
  display size, a fuse from the first dated exchange to today, what they said in their own words,
  no to-do list. Today leads with what waits, red only past a real due date.
- **Sidebar**: All records is the two-way graph; Knowledge keeps its name; Activity and Reviews leave
  the sidebar (both still reachable).

## Install after publication

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.3rc64'
```
