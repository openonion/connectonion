# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc11 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**. Python **3.11+**.

## REM reader

Since 1.9.6rc9 (the first three items already shipped inside rc10, whose notes covered the model change only):

- **Ignore is a word on every open row.** Today's cards, both quiet folds, and every band of Waiting on you
  end with one strip in one order: 标为完成 · 暂不处理 · 忽略 · 交给 Codex · 交给 Claude · 更多. An offline
  snapshot shows the same words, disabled and grey, says once that marks need the running reader, and
  never pretends to save (#3018, #3023).
- **Agenda is a calendar.** It opens on the next seven days with a month view; each night REM saves the
  next three weeks of events from the Google and Outlook calendars you already connected (it never starts
  a login) and puts them beside the notebook's dated promises. A calendar that fails keeps what it saved
  before and says why; on a phone it is a day-by-day list (#3015).
- **Translate every page with one command or one button.** `co rem translate` (or «全部翻译成中文（N 页）»
  in Settings) translates every page not yet in your language — English or mixed — Today's first, stops
  at the quota floor kept for your own work, and never runs alongside the night's translation (#3014).
- **People opens on its table.** Name, organisation, last contact, messages in 90 days, who wrote last,
  open items and page depth, sorted by last contact; a filter on the title line; no Today counts above
  it. On a phone, cards (#3017).

There is nothing to do: install this release and run `co rem open`.
