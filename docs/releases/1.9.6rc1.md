# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc1 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**; Python **3.11+** and the existing dependency floors are retained.

## Today's open reply, on a desktop

- The letter in Today's open reply shows whole lines. Before, its second line was half hidden behind
  the slot question under it; now the window holds three whole lines of the letter, which matches
  four whole lines of the 中文对照 beside it, so neither column shows a cut line (#2923).
- REM's draft summary no longer runs into the "Gmail reply · 7 days" line below it: a long row
  ends in an ellipsis on its own line (#2923).
- On a phone the letter scrolls to its last line instead of stopping behind a fade.

## Today's open reply, on a phone

- The letter starts on the first screen, above the Send bar. Before, with two items on the card,
  REM's question came first and the letter began behind the bar (#2926).
- Send's buttons keep their words whole: "Skip" no longer breaks into "Ski / p", and
  "Write full screen" stays on one line. The hint takes its own line above the buttons.
- The reply's needed parts fit the first screen: the letter, REM's question with its answer box,
  and Send. The other items on the card and each item's details, hand-off and note lines sit under
  **More**; pressing it brings everything back.
- With motion on, the Send bar stays at the bottom of the screen once the card has risen into place.

## Install after publication

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.6rc1'
```
