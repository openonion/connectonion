# 1.9.6rc31

Release candidate for 1.9.6. Stable remains 1.9.5.

## Today and the calendar (#3152)
- The calendar is part of Today: under the action cards, a seven-day strip (weather, dots, multi-day bars) over the chosen day's timeline. The day plan's proposals are dashed "suggested" blocks; the week and month open in place. The separate 日程 rail place is gone; its addresses open Today's calendar.

## Design system, next slice (#3131, #3144)
- Person, organisation, project and knowledge pages: one reading measure (720 px, centred), a flat header (title 24 px semibold, one meta line, the action slot 调查 · ⋯ on the right), hairlines instead of cards.
- The accent is ink (#121512 light, #eef0ed dark) instead of the saturated green; red stays for late items.

## Keys (#3137)
- A 钥匙 / Keys place: every account and key in one place. The value lives in the macOS Keychain, never in the notebook. Copy clears the clipboard after 30 s; the phone shows rows, never values. Import from 1Password, LastPass or Bitwarden CSV. Any page carrying a key-shaped string is refused.

## Tags (#3093)
- Every page kind carries tags (skills, decisions, knowledge and principles included). Each tag has a page, search finds tags, lists and the open items filter by tag (with close-all), the graph shows tags as nodes, and person pages list their tags.

## Faster, and steadier at night
- **Speed (#3106):** approving a Send and opening a talk answer in under a second on a real-sized notebook. Short mail citations resolve from one index, and Today's queue is built once per notebook change.
- **Host upgrades (#3112):** a Host older than the installed release replaces itself on the same port once idle, without `co rem open`.
- **Done marks (#3098):** a done or ignored item stays closed when the night rewords its line; it comes back only with new mail, and says why.
- **Deleted mail (#3101):** a mail deleted from the mailbox is skipped and recorded instead of failing the whole page's investigation.
