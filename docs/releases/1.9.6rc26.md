# 1.9.6rc26

Release candidate for 1.9.6. Stable remains 1.9.5.

REM sprint 22: the problems the owner found by using rc20, fixed and checked by clicking.

## Today

- **Send always answers.** With a slot still open, clicking Send says "fill in the marked part first" and
  focuses it, instead of doing nothing. An accepted send shows "working…", then the time and the recipient,
  with Undo during the 10-minute hold (#3102).
- **Full-screen writing** is a visible button beside Send again, not hidden in the ⋯ menu.
- The **more** panel's "talk with Codex / Claude" actions are one row of 44 px buttons.

## Person, organisation and project pages

- The **profile card** sits directly under the name: contact, work, relationship and identity facts, all
  visible without expanding (#3103).
- **Full memory opens by itself.** Its **sources** have human titles (mail subject and sender, attachment,
  session title, file), are sorted by how often they are cited, show ten first, and link both ways with the
  lines that cite them (#3104).

## Investigate

- `co rem investigate PAGE` writes the page when there is none and **appends** when there is one; a program
  check refuses any draft that drops a line the page had (#3105).
- `--enrich` adds the separate web step; `--rewrite` produces a draft held for approval
  (`--accept-rewrite`, `--discard-rewrite`); `--undo` reverts the last run. `co rem enrich PAGE` now runs
  `investigate --enrich`. The night still runs enrich in its deepen slot.
- Record pages get one **Investigate** button; the line under it says why (new mail not yet in the page,
  leads not followed, never investigated), with progress, what changed, and Undo.

## Also

- The calendar draws events of 24 hours or more as bars in the all-day strip.
- Knowledge opens on the graph; the rail order and the Skills table follow the people-table layout; project
  pages are maps without open-work lists.
