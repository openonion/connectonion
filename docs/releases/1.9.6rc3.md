# ConnectOnion 1.9.6rc3 (release candidate)

Stable remains **1.9.5**. Python **3.11+**. The Onionwright floor rises to **0.0.15**.

## One WTF Browser profile, whoever opens it

On macOS the paid engine used to keep two profiles. The one you got depended on whether the app was
already running when co started:

- **WTF Browser.app is already open** (you double-clicked it): co attaches to it, so co and you share
  `~/Library/Application Support/WTF Browser/profile`.
- **The app is not open**: co started its own copy on a second profile,
  `~/.onionwright/profiles/browser-owned`. That copy also ran with Playwright's `--password-store=basic`
  and `--no-sandbox`.

So a site you signed in to in the app looked signed out to a scheduled job, and the other way round.

With Onionwright 0.0.15 (openonion/onionwright#74), co's own start opens the app's profile. It keeps
the sandbox on and passes no keychain switches, so cookies are read from the same macOS Keychain
entries a double-click uses. If the app is open but has not allowed automation, co now says so,
`browser_owned_profile_held`, instead of starting a second window that exits at once.

**One-time step:** logins stored in the old `~/.onionwright/profiles/browser-owned` are not moved.
Open WTF Browser and sign in to the sites you use once. After that, the app and co are the same
browser.

`$CO_BROWSER_PROFILE_DIR` keeps its meaning: with it set, the paid engine uses
`$CO_BROWSER_PROFILE_DIR/onion` and does not share the app's profile.

## The app in /Applications keeps up

- After a WTF launch on macOS, co now checks the app a person double-clicks in /Applications. If it is
  missing, or is an OpenOnion build of another version or channel, co installs the build it just
  started; an old one goes to the Trash first. It leaves the app alone, and says so, when it is
  open, is not OpenOnion's, or /Applications is not writable (#2932, #2941).

## REM reader

- On a phone, the Send bar now sits directly on the tab bar, so no strip of the page shows between
  them (#2933, the known issue in 1.9.6rc2).

## Install after publication

```bash
uv tool install 'connectonion==1.9.6rc3' --with onionwright --prerelease allow --force
```
