## Source meaning and manual page corrections

The nine complete organization pages were reviewed and corrected manually:

- Different programmes, allocations and offers stay distinct. The useful
  finding is the supported capacity/acceptance condition, with the execution or
  exception outcome qualified when unknown.
- Supplied agreement provisions are labelled as template wording. Incomplete
  fields and blank signatures in a local copy do not establish an executed
  agreement or prove that no later agreement exists. Default ownership and an
  optional signed assignment are not automatically a conflict. The original
  email's proposed venue and the template's venue remain separately qualified.
  This records source wording and uncertainty, not a legal determination.
- Calendar acceptances, bulk notices, copied introductions and student-domain
  authorship do not prove attendance, direct first contact, employment or
  completed development. Original headers replace notebook self-citations and
  support friendly linked contact names.
- Closure, conditional services and historical missing outcomes no longer
  become current overdue work. A recent scope request remains a neutral current
  request; its approval outcome is unknown. A receipt already sent is not an
  unanswered request, and unverified requested fields remain qualified.
- Event dates use the notebook calendar. Sales claims remain attributed
  outreach rather than verified company capabilities. A bulk notice is not
  used as first personal contact.

The organization skill now teaches these boundaries. Its previous long example,
which also implied acceptance without evidence, was replaced with a compact
synthetic example. The first composed instruction gate failed at 16,008
characters; the final maintenance/investigation instructions are 14,765 and
11,417 characters, below the unchanged 15,000 limit.
