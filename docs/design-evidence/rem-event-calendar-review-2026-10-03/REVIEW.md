## Source scope and useful findings

This round reads four actual person pages and their complete evidence. All
four current input sets contain the same eight unique messages: seven direct
participant messages and one marked reaction context. They are related venue
correspondence across two provider threads, not 32 unique originals or one
exact thread. Canonical provider-rendered bodies and structured From/To/Cc
were inspected; cleaned investigation text omits some quoted history.

Authorized read-only historical retrieval obtained all 14 exact previously
cited bodies: three for one person and eleven for another. Bounded participant
listings returned seven and 43 rows. This establishes retrieval of those cited
messages, not complete historical mailbox coverage. Retrieval ended with exit
0; no mail was sent or external event workflow performed. These are fresh
provider-rendered captures, not original MIME or independent observations.

The sources establish useful constraints: a one-time free venue arrangement
left setup and pack-up with organisers while the required onsite role was to
watch; a later change made the full space available for Saturday afternoon.
Another exchange limited a planned reporting change to one named colleague;
their pay-claim process should stay the same, with unusual matters raised to
the coordinator. Historical signatures and a later successor reference show
why an old programme-manager title cannot be assumed current.

Four baseline pages nevertheless said “Nothing open.” Plans, logistics
confirmation and an arrival reply do not prove an event or pack-up completed,
nor establish that nothing remained open months later. Copied recipients do
not become organisers or acquire tasks from those messages. Conversely, missing
historical outcomes do not create present-day debts.
