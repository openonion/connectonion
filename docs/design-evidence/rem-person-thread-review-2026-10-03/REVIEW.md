Candidate in draft PR #2141; tracked in #2185. The independent reviewer is an
AI using a marketing/UI technology-founder perspective, not a human founder.
Private identities, paths, bodies and screenshots are omitted here.

## Discovery and source scope

We triaged the lead, Insight and Open threads of all 36 written person pages.
This was a skim, not a full source or semantic audit. A copied team member's
page still said the client owed scope approval, while the team lead's page
recorded that approval and the later word-limit discussion. Another person's
page reversed the debtor of a client approval request.

The full source audit in this round covers the first person's complete page and
12 complete supplied mail entries: six direct messages and six thread-context
messages. The independent reviewer read all 12 underlying provider-rendered
snapshot bodies, including quoted tails, and matched hashed source identity,
provider, timestamp and structured From/To/Cc for 12/12. Existing quote/noise
cleaning means supplied text is not claimed byte-identical to the full snapshot.
The second person's changed approval clause was checked against the requester’s
original message and reminder. Their other claims were not audited this round.

Three matching replies show the client approving with additions, the team lead
acknowledging it and then raising a word limit. They answer the initial approval
request; they do not establish final wording, submission or the copied member's
personal work. Three other replies on shared poll/install threads concern
different teams and cannot close this team's request.

## Candidate changes and actual correction

Address-only person selection omitted the later approval replies after the
copied member was dropped. Person archive material now includes saved messages
sharing an exact provider/thread identifier with direct indexed messages. It
uses no subject-line fallback or transitive expansion; unsaved bodies and
missing thread IDs add no context.

Additional messages carry a relationship-scope warning and are excluded from
extracted personal contact facts. Thread identity is retrieval scope, not proof
of matching subject, agreement or personal obligation. Writer instructions
require matching the team and ask and distinguishing requester, decision maker
and debtor. Indexed evidence also preserves raw participant metadata, including
Cc, which the provider-rendered body may omit.

Two private pages were manually corrected. The first now leads with the
answered approval and unknown final wording; it assigns no personal task to
the copied member. The second removes the client's approval from the wrong
person's Open threads and keeps it as dated History/Uncertainties, using the
precise co-recipient wording. This prevents the false task appearing in Next
exchanges. The other person's previously recorded outreach item remains outside this source
audit. These manual corrections do not prove automatic compliance.

The read-only index inventory finds 17 of 36 people gain 96 context/page pairs,
at most 34 for one page, with no duplicate context identities. These pairs
overlap across pages; they are not 96 unique conversations or verified findings.
