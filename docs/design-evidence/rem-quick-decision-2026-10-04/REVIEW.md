## What was checked

- A synthetic test places the two sides of a decision in different coding sources, outside both recent-twelve slices. It checks that the quick prompt includes their original IDs and source items, that the comparison packet calls them candidate leads, and that the model still receives no more than 24 ordinary evidence items.
- A local read of the real five-day coding window, with no model call or network mail, found both original decision messages in the new packet. It gathered 29 candidate items from two coding subscriptions before selecting the 24-item model sample.
- A private coding-only `co rem investigate me --quick --days 5` completed in 149 seconds with 95,373 input and 2,012 output tokens. Its coverage reported 70 Codex and 67 Claude Code messages in the window, 12 recent messages per source, four additional original decision turns, then a 24-of-28 quick model sample. The page's lead and Insight named the earlier direction change. The key claim's original coding source was checked and supports it.

## Limits

This was one coding-only quick run on an isolated notebook, not an installed-wheel fresh init. Gmail account reads timed out twice before investigation; a separate Outlook-only attempt reached investigation but source reading timed out twice. The successful run deliberately unsubscribed both mail sources in that scratch notebook. One broader lead sentence carried an additional citation from a different product-init discussion that does not directly support the memory-init decision. No all-page semantic audit, two-mix acceptance, or published-release claim is made here. The page still used the old `investigated` footer because #2178's partial-state change is a separate PR.
