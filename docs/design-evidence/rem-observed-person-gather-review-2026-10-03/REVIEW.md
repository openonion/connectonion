# Later retained mail reaches person investigation material

Candidate in draft PR #2141; automatic reconciliation remains tracked in
#2185. The independent reviewer is an AI using a technology-founder perspective
with marketing and UI experience. No human founder participation is claimed.
Private identities, bodies, native message IDs and screenshots remain private.

## Problem and candidate

The reader could open a retained later reply while the next person investigation
still received only initial-window person references and thread metadata. A
useful source being inspectable did not establish that the writer could use it.
This could preserve an obsolete request after a reply was already saved.

The candidate shares the initial-plus-observed metadata selection between the
reader index and person material. Initial metadata and full snapshots retain
precedence for the same provider/native identity. Additional direct messages
require an exact known email address in the saved From/To/Cc headers, including
when a readable initial manifest exists without an old person index.

Context comes from one-hop, nonempty, exact provider/thread matches seeded by
direct messages. Same subjects, cross-provider thread strings and empty threads
do not join. Context is labelled as potentially concerning someone else;
it is not the person's statement, contact date or obligation. The writer still
has to match the actual request and team within a genuine thread.

Initial continuous coverage remains the manifest's original interval. Retained
individual observations do not fill its gaps, change saved-share counts or
rewrite initial inventories/indexes. Sent, captured and retained times remain
separate; unknown original capture stays unknown. Input and body-format limits
reach the readable evidence packet. Both local coverage labels now say private
mail archive, including the branch with an available provider client.

## Actual source and runtime scope

A repeatable, offline 730-day source-selection assay examined 36 person-page
references, including a generic owner diagnostic with sent-only filtering off.
Material/page pairs increased from 2,279 to 2,427; context/page pairs from 96 to
108. Nine input sets changed and 59 unique sources became newly available.
These are source-selection counts, not semantic acceptance of 36 pages.
The separate owner sent-only gather returned 237 items, all marked as owner
messages; their complete semantics and an automatic owner write were not reviewed.

The two selected contact packets increased from 17 to 39 and from six to 29
messages. All 68 unique full originals were checked against their saved identity,
headers, date and body hash. The independent reviewer read 15 newly included
originals completely and reused the previous round's 53 full-source readings
after exact artifact comparisons. No new provider reads occurred in this round.
Both selected packets contain direct participants only, with zero relationship-
scoped items; they do not prove real dropped-recipient attribution by a writer.

Eighteen body packets preserve the 68 supplied sources, cleaned text and
provenance labels: 140,715 raw decoded characters. Two index files bring the
total to 20 Markdown files and 144,371 raw decoded characters. The canonical saved originals
retain the full provider renderings and quoted chains. Packet availability does
not prove that a model read or correctly used them.

All 36 page bytes, the initial source inventory, archive manifest and two
captured operational cursors remain unchanged. A fresh quota reading was 87%
weekly use against the configured 70% floor. No investigation model call,
quota override, provider switch, release or deployment occurred.
