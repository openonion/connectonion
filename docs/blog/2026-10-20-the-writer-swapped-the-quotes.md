# The writer swapped the quotes

A long page is written in rounds, one part of the material at a time. A round
that drops too many of the quotes already on the page is refused, and the page
goes back to what it was. On the owner's page that happened in two of four
rounds of every run: ten to fifteen minutes of model time each, and nothing
kept.

We compared each refused page with the page before it. The writer had not lost
the quotes by accident. It had replaced them with quotes from its own part. The
round's instructions said "delete what it supersedes" and "the page should get
better, not only longer". A newer quote reads like it supersedes an older one,
so the writer swapped them. One more "loss" was not a loss at all: the same
words, copied from evidence that carried escaped quotation marks.

Now the instructions say that every quote already on the page stays word for
word. When a part settles what a quote was about, the quote stays and the
outcome is added next to it. The check ignores escaping, spacing and case. When
a round still drops a quote, the quoted line is put back next to its rewrite,
the same way project pages already do it. Only a quote that cannot be put back
that way costs one repair turn. If the repair fails too, the round is refused
as before.

On copies of the same notebook, before and after: no refused rounds on the
owner's page in either run, and on the two organisation pages one refused round
each before, none after.

Two other things turned up. The smaller organisation was the owner's own
company. Its domain was on the owner's own address, so every notice the owner
received there counted as the organisation's mail: package publishes, security
alerts, billing. An organisation's mail now has to involve someone on its
domain who is not the owner and not a notice sender. And Outlook was searched
for "mail", the first label of mail.example.ai, instead of the organisation's
name.

The third we got wrong at first. Mapping took 40 minutes, and we blamed the
background thread that builds the session cache for slowing the project scan
from six seconds to thirteen minutes. It was not the scan. The progress log
shows the scan finished in seconds. The thirteen minutes came after it, in
filing typed messages under their projects, which reads every session again.
The cache is built in its own process now, which does no harm, but the fix
that mattered is in the next post.
