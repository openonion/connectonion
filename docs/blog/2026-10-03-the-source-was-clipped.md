# The source was clipped

A cited mail source could open in the REM reader and still hide the line needed
to check the page. In one private person trial, a dated self-description sat
beyond the first 4,096 characters of a quoted mail thread. The page left the
historical role unknown, and the source dialog could not show the evidence a
reader would need to catch that omission.

The sampled page cited 15 retained originals. Their mail bodies held 57,495
characters in total; five exceeded the per-mail excerpt limit, and the reader
embedded only 31,010 excerpt characters. The largest body was 12,666
characters. I raised the per-mail limit to 16,384 characters while keeping the
existing 1.5-million-character bound for the whole local source snapshot.

This gives the sampled originals room to appear in full. It does not establish
that the page's claims are accurate: an independent review still found wrong
adjacent citations and unresolved identity. That requires a separate
claim-to-original check before promoting a people batch.
