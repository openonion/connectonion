# The labels the translation missed

The reader's Chinese mode translates the interface, and cached translations
cover the pages. We went through a copy of a real notebook page by page:
home, Waiting on you, People, a person, a project and Memory changes, at
desktop and phone width, light and dark, in English and Chinese. Most of
what we found was English that the Chinese mode had missed.

The overnight change on the home page came straight from the run log, so
"Partnerships lead → Head of Partnerships" stayed English under a Chinese
heading. The reader cannot call a model, but it often has the answer
already. When the new value still matches the page's Facts line, the
reader shows that line from the cached translation. The old value has no
translation to draw on, so it keeps its English with a small 原文 mark,
like a page that has not been translated yet.

The Facts panel had a dozen labels with no Chinese entry ("Time zone",
"How we know them", "Signing entity") and "— not found" for every empty
field. Translated prose still said "Unknown" mid-sentence, because the
translator keeps that word in English on purpose: the reader's parser
looks for it to tell an open thread from a settled one. The parser still
sees "Unknown". The reader now displays it as 未知 in Chinese mode.
Summaries in lists began with the Insight labels "Now:" or "Pattern:",
which the week list already stripped and the other lists did not. One
function strips them everywhere now.

The rest was spacing and noise. A person with three titles had all three
in the line under their name. That line now shows the first title and
"+2", with the full list on hover. The overnight row said "supported" and
"source" one after the other; it keeps the source link only. The recall
button had a dashed border that looked unfinished, and the page's
disclosures came in three different sizes. On a phone, the "+" of Full
memory and sources dropped to a line of its own.
