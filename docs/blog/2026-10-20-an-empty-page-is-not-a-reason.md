# An empty page is not a reason

Left to choose for itself on a fresh copy of a real notebook, deepen picked three projects and one person. The projects grew between three and nine times. The person had sent five mails, and their page was almost empty, which is exactly why the picker chose them. A round on them added one quote. Twenty people who write to the owner far more often waited behind them.

The picker multiplies how much activity there is behind a page by how thin the page is. With both terms weighted equally, an empty page about nobody in particular could outrank a half-written page about the owner's co-founder. Now the activity term is squared. On the same notebook, the people it picks have 274, 55, 30 and 43 mails, and the five-mail person drops to sixtieth.

The first organisation it had picked was an event platform. Its only "person" was a one-way stream of registrations. An organisation whose people are all automated or one-way is now not a candidate. Its page still exists, because initialisation wrote it, but deepening won't spend a round on it.

A slot also stops starting new pages after an hour. One round on a long project history took about an hour by itself. Two such rounds in one slot would run into the next scheduled slot.
