# 1.9.2b25: a note in your own voice

*2026-10-13*

The mail your colleague gets now reads like you wrote it: a line on what you
were working on, a button to see it, and one line to paste into Codex, signed
with your name, from your own mailbox. When they open it, your conversation
appears in their Codex app with each message labelled with your name and the
time you wrote it.

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.2b25'
```
