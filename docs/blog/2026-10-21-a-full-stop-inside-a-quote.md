# A full stop inside a quote

The code that takes a hedge out of a line splits the line into clauses and drops the one that says what is not known. It split at every full stop, including the one inside “The budget is not confirmed. I will know Friday.”, and the first half of the quote held the hedge words, so it went. "Dr. Lin" was a clause boundary too.

Now quoted words are set aside before the split and put back after it, so a quote is always whole and its words are the speaker's, never a hedge to remove. Titles and initials do not end a clause, the bullet and its date are never part of one, and a line whose quote marks do not pair is left as it is. On every hedged line of a real notebook, 217 of them, no quote and no date changed.
