# 1.9.3rc9: pages that quote people

*2026-10-20*

Most of this candidate is REM. Over the last few days the person pages a first
run writes went from paraphrasing people to quoting them. On notebook copies,
mean quotes per person page rose from 6.8 to 22.4. Most of it came from moving
rules out of prose and into code: the quote floor, the first-contact date, the
order of History, and pronouns that would be read as the owner.

The rest is depth and speed. Enrich reads a long project history one period at
a time, enrich and muse are judged only on the lines they wrote, and gathering
at sixteen workers is about twice as fast.

The posts from 2026-10-20 tell each step, starting with
[The page retold what it could quote](2026-10-20-the-page-retold-what-it-could-quote.md).

One handoff change comes along: a handoff pasted into a conversation that was
already about something else now stops and asks for a new one.

A full first run with everything combined has not finished yet, so treat this
as a preview.
