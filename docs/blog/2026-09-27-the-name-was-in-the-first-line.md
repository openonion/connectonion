# The name was in the first line

The morning's preview gave the Wiki a budget. The owner opened People and found
another problem: page after page titled `larryleework7@gmail.com`,
`vivianshi0511@gmail.com`, `ivanxzhu@gmail.com`. "The mail has their names on
it," they said. "Why am I looking at addresses?"

For most of these people, the mail didn't have their names. They were people
the owner had written to who hadn't written back yet, and the owner had typed
only their address. We checked the address books too. They named four of the
195.

The name was in the first line of the owner's own mail: "Hi Larry,". Gmail and
Outlook already return that line when they list messages, so reading it costs
nothing. The map reads it now. Run against the owner's real mailbox, it named 176 of
those 195 people and changed no name the map already had. Before, the map
only read the headers. The owner's own writing turned out to be the better
source.
