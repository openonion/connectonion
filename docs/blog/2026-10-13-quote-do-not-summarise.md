# Quote, do not summarise

*2026-10-13*

Every time we make a long thing short (a session compacted, a conversation
handed to someone else, a mailbox read for a page), the easy move is to have a
model write a summary. The summary reads well, and it is wrong in a way
nobody can see: it leaves out what the model did not think mattered, and there
is no trace of what went.

So it is now a principle, written into decision 076 next to context over
control: keep the original words and quote them, with where each came from. A
summary may sit beside the quotes as a guide, never instead of them. When the
whole does not fit, cut whole items and say how many were cut and where the
full copy is.

A quote can be checked against its source. A summary can only be trusted.
