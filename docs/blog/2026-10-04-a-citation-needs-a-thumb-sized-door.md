# A citation needs a thumb-sized door

The small number after a REM Skill finding points to an archived source. On a
phone, that number was easy to see but hard to tap. **View sources** offered a
larger route into the page's evidence, yet it made the reader leave the exact
claim they wanted to check.

A cited Skill finding now has a row of larger numbered source links directly
under its lead statement. Each opens the corresponding archived original.
Several links wrap on narrow screens. The existing View sources action and
Full memory stay available, so the short lead is still a way into the record
rather than a substitute for it.

The keyboard focus ring is visible against the dark lead card. If a finding
is marked personal or sensitive, hiding labelled passages hides its direct
links too. This is a reader change; the source text and written memory are
unchanged.

We checked an invented Skill and one read-only real Skill sample at phone and
desktop widths. The [preview notes](/releases/1.9.0a39) describe the bounded
check. A larger tap target helps a reader reach the original; it does not
verify the finding for them.
