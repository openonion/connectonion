# Suppose the receipts have already gone

In the sample notebook, Inès is waiting for Q3 receipts. Open Today on the previous preview and her card shows the balance of the correspondence in a few lines. She files the return soon. Avery owes her the receipts. REM has even written the reply, down to the choice between attaching them and sharing a folder.

For the release check I opened that card in Chrome at 1440 pixels wide. Send was easy to find. Then I read the card a second time with one detail changed in my head. Suppose Avery had already sent the receipts from Outlook that morning. Where would they click now?

The name, address and subject were all correct. The missing thing was an ordinary way to say the work was done. A test that picked a delivery method and pressed Send could pass every time while missing Avery's situation entirely.

That became the starting point for the preview comparison. Use the same invented correspondent and the same letter on both builds. Look first for what Avery can do without sending another email. Only then open the writer and see what the recipient would get. The fixture intercepts the mail request, so we can check the exact words crossing that boundary without putting an extra message in anybody's inbox.
