# A note from someone you know

*2026-10-13*

Aaron sent himself a handoff and read it the way a stranger would. It came
from an agent mailbox he had never seen, under the subject
`[co handoff] Fix slugify in slug.py per docs team rules (from Aaron Test)`.
It opened with a ConnectOnion banner and every finding laid out. He said he
would not have trusted it enough to paste anything into Codex.

A handoff mail is the first thing someone learns about your work, and about
co. So it now reads like a note from you, and comes from you.

- **From your own mailbox.** If the AI running `co handoff send` has a mail
  tool, it sends the mail itself. Otherwise co uses your Gmail or Outlook,
  if you have connected one. The agent's mailbox is the last resort, and the
  preview tells you when it would be used. There is no option to choose:
  the best mailbox you have is picked for you.
- **Short.** "Aaron Xie passed you some work: Fix slugify". One or two
  sentences on who worked on what with which AI, and what they talked through.
  Then the block to paste into a new Codex conversation. What was decided
  arrives with the handoff when their agent accepts, not in the mail.
- **In your language.** A session held in Chinese sends a Chinese mail.
