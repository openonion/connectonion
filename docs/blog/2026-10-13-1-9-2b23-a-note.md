# 1.9.2b23: a note from someone you know

*2026-10-13*

The handoff mail now comes from your own mailbox (your AI's mail tool, or your
Gmail or Outlook) and reads like a note from you, in the language you worked
in: who passed what, a sentence on what you discussed, and one paragraph to
paste into Codex. `co handoff open` keeps the session going until the work is
done.

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade 'connectonion==1.9.2b23'
```
