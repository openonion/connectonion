# 1.9.2b16: the first handoff reads like an introduction

When you hand work to someone who has never used ConnectOnion, the email they
get is their first impression of it. In this preview that email says who you
are, what the task is, where it stands and what is still open, before it asks
them to do anything.

Then it asks for one thing: paste a single block, marked where it starts and
ends, into Codex or Claude Code. Their agent installs what it needs, tells you
they have it, and explains the task to them before it touches a file. The email
holds together in Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook, and on a phone.

Behind it, the agent on the other side now hears every answer you send, even
one that arrives before it starts listening.
