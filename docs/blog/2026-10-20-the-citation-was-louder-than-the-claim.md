# The citation was louder than the claim

A person's page in the reader opens with two sentences of lead. On the
invented fixture, Mara Ostrowski's lead carried five citations, each drawn as
a rounded pill: "mara, Outlook, 10 Oct". At 1440 pixels one pill wrapped to the
start of a line by itself, with the sentence's full stop left stranded after
it. The evidence was there, but the reader had to step over it to read the
claim.

Citations now sit after the word they support as a small raised date, such as
"10 Oct" or "9 Sep +1", in the quiet grey we use for metadata. Hovering shows
the full source, and a click still opens the source panel. The marker is
tethered to the word before it and to the full stop after it, so neither can
wrap away. History rows keep the full chips, because there the citation is the
row's point.

The source panel used to say only that excerpts were hidden when labelled
passages were off, which is the default. It now quotes the page line the
citation supports and shows the sender, channel and time, and puts the switch
for labelled passages right there. The excerpt itself stays behind the privacy
rule.

The reader's interface can now be shown in Chinese. It starts in the owner's first
detected language, or the browser's when the notebook has not detected one,
and an "EN | 中文" switch next to the theme remembers the choice. Only the
interface is translated; pages keep their own words, and English dates are
formatted exactly as before. The first attempt used the browser locale for English too,
which turned "Sep" into "Sept" and broke two timezone tests, so English kept
its old formatter.

On a phone, the People list was a table squeezed to 390 pixels; on a real
notebook with several addresses per person it was 317 pixels wider than the
screen. It is now a list of cards with one sort menu. On the desktop table,
emails show the first address and a "+N", after a first attempt let them run
into the Phone column.

Smaller things: an organisation page no longer repeats its first open thread
above the Open threads list, connected-context cards stop repeating each
other's snippets, and the empty Agent sessions page explains itself instead of
printing a long command.
