# One date, once

An organisation's page showed the date of its last activity twice: once in
the line above the title, and again under it with "3 days ago". The facts
strip further down repeated it a third time as an ISO date. Project pages
did the same with the last mapped session. Each date now appears once,
under the title, in the reader's own date format. The file's last update
moved into the header of Full memory and sources, where the rest of the
page's bookkeeping already lives.

On a person's page, "Between you" put a thick amber bar over what you owe
and a thick black bar over what they owe. The bars were the heaviest marks
on the page, heavier than the facts they framed. Both columns now share
one thin rule. The direction shows in the count's colour, and each
thread's age sits at its top right, amber for what you owe, as it does on
Home. On project and organisation pages the age or due date used to float
in the middle of the row; it now sits at the row's right edge as well.

In Chinese mode, the Connected context cards quoted the linked page's line
in English even when that page had a cached translation. The reader now
finds the same line in the translation. When it cannot, the card shows
the English with a 原文 mark. A few sentences the reader writes itself, such as
"The supplied session sample did not confirm current project work", had
never been passed to the translation table, so they stayed English too.

One thing we left alone: a person's lead ends with "Last contact:" and a
date, and the line under it repeats the date. The page writer puts that
sentence there, and the notebook's index reads it, so it stays in the page.
