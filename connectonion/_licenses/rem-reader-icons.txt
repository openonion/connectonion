Icons in the REM reader (connectonion/rem/reader.html, the ICONS map)

Every picture in ICONS is copied from one of two published sets; none is drawn by hand.
The path data is the published file's, with two mechanical changes: Lucide's <rect> elements
are written as the same rectangle in path form (the reader's style sheet owns every radius
attribute), and the reader's own style sheet sets the stroke (1.7 wide, round caps and joins).
Service marks keep their published shape, are painted in the page's text colour and are
drawn at 20/24 of the box so they match the outline icons optically.
